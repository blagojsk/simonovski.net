About

I'm a system architect with more than a decade of experience designing cloud-native, event-driven and AI-augmented platforms for aviation, media, logistics, education and workforce scheduling. My work spans the whole lifecycle — pre-sales consulting and rapid prototyping, architecture and infrastructure design, hands-on Java and Spring Boot development, and DevOps automation on AWS, Azure and Google Cloud. Lately that means PostgreSQL, vector databases and LLM-backed systems. Along the way I lead cross-functional teams, mentor developers and build proof-of-concepts that turn open technical questions into decisions.
